Interpretation and limitations

Repeated timing differences are a signal for further investigation, not a diagnosis. A reachability problem does not automatically mean a service was blocked by an ISP.

CDN placement, service load, peering, routing, DNS, VPNs, proxies, Zscaler or other corporate gateways, firewalls, browser extensions, temporary congestion, outages, rate limiting, and actual traffic management can all affect results.

Browser-only PingMetric cannot perform low-level packet replay like a native controlled Wehe client. This heuristic cannot establish provider intent or a legal violation. Repeating tests at different times can provide more context. No result guarantees that all Internet traffic is treated equally.
